# Melody credits

The nine Cal Bar melodies are original synthesized sequences. Two additional
single-line instrumental excerpts use early compositions by **Sayed Darwish
(1892–1923)**: **Salma Ya Salama** and **El Helwa Di**.

Cal Bar synthesizes the notes itself with its own timbres and reminder tempos.
It includes no lyrics, sampled recordings, or backing tracks. Salma is based
on Darwish's tune, rather than Jeff Barnel's later composition popularized by Dalida.

References consulted for melody identification and notation:

- Cambridge Takht's Darwish tribute booklet (history and composer attribution):
  https://www.cambridgetakht.com/darwish
- El Helwa Di melody staff, GRIMMWELT songbook, printed page 55:
  https://www.grimmwelt.de/files/downloads/U_Erzaehl-mir-deine-Geschichten/GRIMMWELT-Kassel_Musikalische_Einheiten_Liederheft1.pdf
- Salma Ya Salama, Darwish melody staff, notation by رحاب رحاب:
  https://musescore.com/user/29153492/scores/7103256

Only the underlying melody is used; the references' instrumentation, harmonies,
engraving, and recordings are not bundled. The original compositions date from
Darwish's lifetime; copyright status can vary by jurisdiction.
